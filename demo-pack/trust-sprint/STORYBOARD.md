# Trust Sprint demo: storyboard

- **Audience:** Head of Data evaluating text-to-SQL or Cortex Analyst for business users.
- **Outcome:** they see which business questions are safe to hand to AI before rollout, and exactly why the others are not.
- **Length:** about 45 seconds, 16:9 master plus a vertical cut and a hero image.
- **App state:** Snowflake & dbt Auditor, `/trust` page, bundled report `lib/trust-sample.json`, generated by the Trust Sprint toolkit running `scripts/trust-jaffle/` against the public dbt jaffle-shop on Snowflake, with 2 defects seeded on purpose. The page labels it "recorded real run" and names the seeded defects; the video keeps that label on screen.

## Scenes

1. **Inputs.** Open `/trust` on section 01. Proof: the recorded-run banner and the five metrics with owner and status (Coupon Share flagged). Caption: "The inputs: five business metrics, each with an owner".
2. **Evaluation.** Scroll to 02. Proof: five business questions with pass, warning and fail, and the reason for each. Caption: "Five business questions, run on Snowflake one by one".
3. **Risk matrix.** Scroll to 03. Proof: score 74, 1 error, 1 warning, and the two findings. Caption: "One score for the domain, and exactly what drags it down". Hero image here.
4. **Recommendations.** Scroll to 04. Proof: define Churn Rate in the trust map first; guard the Coupon Share denominator. Caption: "What to fix before anyone asks the AI".
5. **Evidence.** Scroll to 05. Proof: the checks that ran and the report download. Caption: "Every result is traceable to the toolkit run".

End card: "AI Analytics Trust Sprint, 5 days, fixed scope. the brand site".

## Honesty rules

- Narrate only what the screen proves. No accuracy percentages, no client names.
- The data is a recorded run on public data, the two seeded defects are named, and the screen says so in every scene.
